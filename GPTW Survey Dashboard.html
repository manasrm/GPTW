Key Insights & Intelligence
Management Trust Is Eroding Fast
Multiple management-related scores declined 15–19 points vs Feb 2025. "Management competent" fell from 90 → 71 (▼19). "Actions match words" dropped from 80 → 62 (▼18). This is a systemic trust crisis, not isolated feedback.
Compensation Perception Is the #1 Pain Point
"Fair share of profits" scores only 51 — the lowest single statement company-wide, down 16 pts. "Paid fairly" fell from 70 → 59. This poses immediate retention risk, particularly in US ToD Delivery and Client Services.
Collaboration & Impartiality: Company-wide Weak Spots
Both categories are in the bottom 3 across ALL BUs. Collaboration averages 58 company-wide (45 in US ToD). Impartiality averages 57 (46 in US ToD). These are structural gaps, not isolated BU issues.
Frontline Manager Effectiveness Gap
Executives score 88% while Frontline Managers score only 67%. Complaints around favoritism (59), recognition gaps (64), and manager communication (63) point to inconsistent middle-management practices as a root cause.
D&I Scores Remain a Genuine Strength
Fairness across gender (85), race (84), age (83), and sexual orientation (82) are among the highest scores company-wide. This is a competitive employer branding advantage worth amplifying in talent acquisition.
Digital Engineering Is a Beacon of Excellence
With an average of 83, Digital Engineering outperforms every other BU by 13+ points. Its management practices, team culture, and engagement strategies should be documented and cascaded as internal best practices.
Remote Employees Are Happiest (91% avg)
Permanent remote employees score 91% — far above office-based staff. Vadodara office scores only 66%. This gap may reflect environment, management style, or policy differences. Flexible work expansion could be a quick win.
Tenure Risk: Mid-Career Employees Disengaging
New employees (<2 yrs) score highest at 78%, with scores falling over time. The employee experience degrades post-onboarding — pointing to unmet growth, recognition, and career development expectations.

Recommended Action Plan

Priority 1 — Critical  (Immediate Action Required)

P1
Compensation & Profit Sharing
Review compensation structure company-wide. Benchmark against industry standards. Introduce transparent profit-sharing or bonus programs. Communicate salary bands clearly. Target: raise "paid fairly" score from 59 → 70+.
P1
Management Credibility & Trust Restoration
Establish quarterly town halls and "Ask Me Anything" sessions with leadership. Create a public commitment tracker for promises. Mandate decisions affecting employees be communicated with rationale within 48 hours.
P1
US ToD Delivery — Comprehensive Culture Audit
Commission an independent culture review (scored 60, Collaboration at 45, Impartiality at 46). Conduct confidential focus groups, review management practices, create a 90-day improvement plan with measurable milestones.
P1
Employee Involvement in Decisions
Create cross-functional advisory councils by BU. Implement a structured feedback loop where employee suggestions receive a response within 2 weeks. Score dropped 13 pts — this is a key driver of disengagement.

Priority 2 — Important  (30–90 Day Focus)

P2
Frontline Manager Development Program
Launch a structured manager excellence program covering unconscious bias, recognition practices, team communication, and decision transparency. Implement 360° feedback for all people managers.
P2
Fair Promotions & Career Ladders
Publish clear promotion criteria and career progression frameworks for all roles. Create a transparent promotion panel review process. Address "promotions go to deserving" score of 60 (▼9 pts).
P2
Recognition Programs
Implement a peer-to-peer recognition platform. Create monthly spotlight programs and milestone-based recognition. Address "opportunity for special recognition" score of 64 (▼15 pts).
P2
Gender Pay Gap & Equity Audit
Conduct an equity audit addressing the 9-point gender score gap (Males 75% vs Females 66%). Review pay equity across levels, locations, and genders. Publish an Equity Report to demonstrate commitment.
P2
Job Security Communication
Share company growth roadmap and financial health updates. Directly address layoff concerns. "Layoffs as last resort" dropped 17 pts — silence is amplifying employee anxiety across BUs.

Priority 3 — Maintain & Strengthen  (Ongoing)

P3
Replicate Digital Engineering Excellence
Document Digital Engineering's cultural and management practices (avg 83). Create a "Playbook of Excellence" and run cross-BU learning sessions. Use its leaders as internal culture champions.
P3
Physical Safety & Facilities
Continue safety protocols and regular audits. Budget for facility upgrades, especially in Vadodara (66%) where conditions may be contributing to lower scores.
P3
D&I Programs
Amplify Aditi's genuine D&I strength (gender 85, race 84, age 83) in employer branding. Use scores in recruitment marketing. Continue and expand inclusion initiatives.
P3
Mid-Career Experience Program
Build on strong onboarding (87) with a "Year 2–3 Experience" program to retain mid-tenure talent. Assign career coaches at the 12-month mark. Address the post-honeymoon engagement drop.
